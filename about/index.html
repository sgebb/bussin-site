About Bussin

Bussin is a modern Azure Service Bus interface hosted at app.bussin.dev. It gives teams a polished dark UI for monitoring queues, topics, subscriptions, and message payloads without leaving the browser.

What Bussin does

  • Visualize and manage queues, topics, subscriptions, and outgoing messages.
  • Filter, search, and inspect messages quickly with rich context.
  • Support Service Bus workflows with a clean dark interface and intuitive controls.

Why it matters

Bussin helps teams work faster on Azure Service Bus operations by making message flows and resource state easier to understand at a glance.
