Glossary

Dead-letter queue

A dead-letter queue holds Azure Service Bus messages that could not be processed normally. Bussin.dev exposes dead-letter inspection and recovery directly in the browser.

Why messages go to the dead-letter queue

Messages are dead-lettered for delivery failures, expiration, filter mismatch, or manual route decisions. The dead-letter queue keeps the message body and metadata for investigation.

How to inspect dead-lettered messages

View dead-letter reason, exception details, and user properties before choosing whether to replay or archive the message.

Why Bussin helps

Bussin.dev lets you open dead-letter queues directly in the browser and move selected messages back to an active queue or subscription with explicit recovery controls.
