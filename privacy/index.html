Privacy

Privacy policy for Bussin.dev

This marketing site is a static product and documentation hub for app.bussin.dev. It does not collect personal data directly, and browser-based Service Bus access is only performed with explicit user consent in the app.

Information collection

We currently do not collect personal information through this static marketing site. Any analytics or external tools added later will be disclosed here.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies are set by this site itself. If third-party analytics are added later, they will be documented in this policy.

App behavior and data

app.bussin.dev connects to Azure Service Bus directly from the browser after you sign in and consent. The app does not act autonomously or store Service Bus message content outside of the browser session unless otherwise disclosed.

Preferences like theme and favorites are stored in the browser only.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Bussin team through the app or via public project channels.
