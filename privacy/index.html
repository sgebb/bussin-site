Privacy Policy

This site is a static marketing site for app.bussin.dev and does not collect personal data directly. Standard analytics or service provider tools must be declared separately if they are added in the future.

Information collection

Currently, this marketing site does not gather personal information from visitors. It is designed to share public content only.

Cookies and tracking

If third-party analytics or tracking tools are integrated later, those details will be added here.

App behavior and data

app.bussin.dev uses browser-based auth and direct Azure Service Bus connections. Bussin does not act on its own, and it only moves or inspects data when you explicitly choose to do so.

We do not store Azure Service Bus message or namespace content outside of basic analytics tied to the site. Preferences such as favorites and light/dark mode are stored in the browser only.

Bussin is open source. You can inspect the client and authentication flow at github.com/sgebb/bussin.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app.bussin.dev team.
