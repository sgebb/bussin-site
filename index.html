Browser-native Azure Service Bus explorer for direct AMQP workflows

Bussin.dev connects your browser directly to Azure Service Bus over AMQP WebSockets. No desktop install, no backend proxy, and no extra runtime — just fast, cross-platform queue, dead-letter, and subscription inspection.

Browser-native architecture

Bussin.dev runs as a static browser client and opens AMQP sessions over secure WebSockets to Azure Service Bus. That means the app can be served globally while the messaging connection stays direct and protocol-native.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bussin.dev need a backend proxy?

No. Bussin.dev connects directly to Azure Service Bus using AMQP over WebSockets from the browser.

Can I use it on Linux and macOS?

Yes. The browser-native design means the same experience works across Mac, Linux, and Windows.

Is this suitable for debugging live Service Bus workflows?

Yes. Bussin.dev focuses on queue inspection, peek-lock message review, dead-letter analysis, and subscription troubleshooting.
