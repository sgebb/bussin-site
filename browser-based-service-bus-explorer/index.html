How it Works

Bussin runs entirely in your browser. By connecting directly to Azure, it eliminates the need for intermediate proxy servers, keeping your connections fast and your data private.

Read Documentation

Direct Browser-to-Broker Connections

Most web-based messaging tools run your data through their own backend servers. This can add latency, cost, and security risks by exposing your messaging payloads to third-party hosts.

Bussin connects directly to Azure Service Bus using standard browser protocols:

  • WebSockets Connection: Azure Service Bus supports connections over WebSockets on port 443. Bussin connects directly to your namespace using this standard protocol.
  • Local Authentication: Sign-in is handled in your browser using Microsoft's official library (MSAL.js). Your access tokens are kept only in temporary browser memory.
  • No Backend Server: All connection details and messages are processed locally in your browser. No message data ever goes to our servers.

Messaging Operations

Since Bussin connects directly to the Service Bus broker, it supports all standard messaging operations:

  • Resource Discovery: Automatically lists all namespaces, queues, and topics you have permission to access.
  • Message Peeking: Read messages without locking them or deleting them from the queue.
  • Peek-Lock Workflows: Lock messages, inspect them, and explicitly choose to complete, abandon, or dead-letter them.
  • Dead-Letter Recovery: Read failed messages and resubmit them back to the active queue.
  • Batching: Send or receive multiple messages efficiently without blocking the interface.

Security

Secure Login

Uses standard OAuth 2.0 PKCE login flows. Your session tokens stay securely in browser memory.

Direct Connections

Uses your browser's network stack to open secure WebSocket connections directly to Azure.

No Data Storage

We do not store your credentials or messages. Everything is kept in temporary memory and is cleared when you close the tab.

Local Settings

Your bookmarks, folders, and settings are saved locally in your browser's storage and are never uploaded.
