Feature overview

Cross-platform Azure Service Bus client

Bussin.dev delivers a consistent Service Bus experience on Mac, Linux, and Windows through the browser. No installation, no native runtime, and no platform-specific distribution.

Open App

Platform-neutral architecture

Using browser-native APIs and AMQP over WebSockets, Bussin.dev avoids the compatibility gaps of native desktop tools, while preserving the capabilities needed for Service Bus workflows.

Developer workflows

  • Inspect queues and subscriptions from any workstation
  • Share the same diagnostic workflow across teams
  • Reduce platform-specific support overhead
