Launching the Bussin static homepage

May 15, 2026 · Marketing · SEO

The new Bussin site is designed to support app.bussin.dev with a clean landing page, blog infrastructure, and SEO-ready content pages.

Why a static homepage?

Static sites load quickly, are easy to audit for SEO, and make it simple to publish clear brand messaging without unnecessary complexity.

What this launch includes

  • A homepage with marketing copy and blog promotion.
  • A blog index and first post for future updates.
  • Dedicated SEO pages like about, privacy, sitemap, and robots rules.
  • A GitHub Pages workflow for automatic deployment.

Next steps are publishing additional content, linking to app.bussin.dev, and continuing the blog with launch updates and SEO topics.
